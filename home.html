Welcome

The FIFA Men's Soccer World Cup 2026 will take place across Canada, the USA and Mexico from 11th June to 19th July 2026. This guide is here to help tourists plan their trip to the tournament.

Host Countries

  • USA - 11 venues including MetLife Stadium (New Jersey), SoFi Stadium (Los Angeles) and AT&T Stadium (Dallas)
  • Canada - 2 venues: BC Place (Vancouver) and BMO Field (Toronto)
  • Mexico - 3 venues including the iconic Estadio Azteca (Mexico City), which will host the Opening Match

What This Guide Offers

  • Stadium Map - Explore all 11 World Cup venues on an interactive map. Click any marker to see venue details, photos and fixture information.
  • Travel Tools - Translate text into Spanish, French and other languages to help you communicate at your destination.

Key Dates

  • Opening Match: 11th June 2026 - Estadio Azteca, Mexico City
  • Final: 19th July 2026 - MetLife Stadium, New Jersey
